隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-11-01

算記（FormuScript，以下稱「本 App」）由 MagicSpark Studio 開發。本政策說明本 App 如何處理你的資料。

一句話說明

本 App 不收集、不上傳、不分享任何個人資料。所有計算、筆記、圖片與錄音只存在你的裝置上。本 App 沒有網路連線權限，不含廣告與追蹤工具。

資料 用途 存放位置
計算記錄、變數、設定 計算機功能 本 App 的私有儲存空間
筆記、標籤 筆記功能 本 App 的私有儲存空間

這些資料不會離開你的裝置，除非你自己使用下列功能：

  • 匯出與備份：你選擇匯出筆記、建立備份 ZIP（含自訂公式）時，檔案會存到你指定的位置。
  • 分享錯誤記錄：App 異常結束後，下次開啟會詢問你是否分享錯誤記錄。只有你按下「分享」並在系統分享選單選擇對象（例如 Email）時才會送出。錯誤記錄只包含錯誤訊息、App 版本、手機型號與 Android 版本，不含你的筆記或計算內容。

解除安裝本 App 時，上述資料會一併由系統刪除。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

FormuScript (算記, "the app") is developed by MagicSpark Studio. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

In short

The app does not collect, upload or share any personal data. All calculations, notes, images and recordings stay on your device. The app has no internet permission and contains no ads or trackers.

Data stored on your device

Data Purpose Location
Calculation history, variables, settings Calculator App-private storage
Notes and labels Notes App-private storage
Photos, images, handwriting and PDF pages you add to notes Note attachments App-private storage
Custom formulas and their pictures Custom tools App-private storage
Voice recordings Voice memos in notes App-private storage
Error log Records why the app closed unexpectedly App-private storage

This data never leaves your device unless you use one of these features yourself:

  • Export and backup: when you export notes or create a backup ZIP (including custom formulas), the file is saved where you choose.
  • Share error log: after an unexpected close, the app asks whether you want to share the error log. It is sent only if you tap "Share" and pick a destination (for example, email) in the system share sheet. The log contains only the error message, app version, phone model and Android version — not your notes or calculations.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of the above.

Permissions

Permission Purpose When it is used
Microphone Voice memos in notes Only when you tap "Record"
Vibration Key-press feedback When you press keys

Taking photos, picking images and importing PDFs use the system camera and file picker; the app only receives the single file you choose and cannot read your gallery or other files.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
